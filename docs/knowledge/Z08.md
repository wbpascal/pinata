## 1. Facts other items need

- **Empty handlers** (profiler enter/leave only, read in the disassembly): 0x383C NC_PARTY_LOGIN_CMD, 0x3846
  NC_PARTY_LOGOUT_CMD, 0xB01D / 0xB01F RAID_ZONE_LOGIN/LOGOUT (FUN_00509900), 0x5C0A / 0x5C0C WT DB answers,
  0x74F4 NC_GUILD_TOURNAMENT_GOLD_REFUND_ZONE_CMD; `so_ply_HolyPromiseSet` (0x9432 / 0x9435) is an empty function.
- **Instance-dungeon key** = `{CATEGORY, RegisterNumber}`: party → `{0, partyNo}`, guild → `{1, guildNo}`, raid →
  `{3, raidNo}` (`ShinePlayer_GetPartyKey` FUN_005605f0, `IDCondition` FUN_004624d0). Party dismiss / raid dismiss / guild
  delete delete the group's dungeons (`MapClusterManager_DeleteDungeon` FUN_004866b0); raid creation moves the first party's
  dungeons to the raid (`ChangeOwner` FUN_00485210); a member who leaves while inside is linked out (`InstanceEjectAfterLeave`
  FUN_00486c00).
- **Quest system** is the shared quest library (`CQuest` on the client, `CQuestZone` on the zone); the script runs on the
  zone and drives the client with 0x4401 commands (`QUEST_SCRIPT_COMMAND` enum, pdb2017). Quest DB requests carry the
  `ref` and answer with 0xB41 on success. Accepting a quest is logged to GameLog with 0xC80F NC_SYSLOG_CHAR_QUEST_STARTED.
  At most 40 diary entries can be accepted (error 0xC0F).
- **KQ handles** sent to the client are XOR-masked with the player's WM handle (`EncHandle` in 0x581A).
  Players listed in the WM's joiner list are pulled into the KQ map automatically at 0, 2, 5, 7 and 10 s after 0x580F.
  0x5833 sends a banned KQ player to Roumen ("RouN", 6445/8630, hard-coded).
- **Guild creation fee**: on 0x7448 NC_GUILD_ZONE_MAKE_CMD the zone takes 1,000,000 cen from the master (money lock +
  apply). The WM / DB do not take it (D04 / W03 can confirm).
- **Friend points** are checked and deducted by the Character DB (0x34BF → 0x34C0); the zone only draws the item from
  `FriendPointReward.shn` (columns InxName, Lot, FPR_Rate) and locks a free cell.
- **Weapon licence slots**: 3 per weapon `{u16 mobId, u32 killCount}`; licence-clear item `DAT_14d50498`.
- **Title change**: 10-second cooldown (`p+0x2aa48`), modes 1–2 only; DB is told with 0x6008 (no answer).
- **Holy promise**: the zone forwards the reward requests to the DB without checks and adds the withdrawn money with
  no upper bound; the DB answers are matched by handle only (Z08-B14).
- 15 original-server bugs, Z08-B1…B15, table in Z08-content §14 (main ones: 0xB01C charNo-as-raidNo, 0x5C02 `bSet`
  uninitialised, 0x74E6 forwards stale bytes, holy-promise money by handle only, level-select flag not cleared).
