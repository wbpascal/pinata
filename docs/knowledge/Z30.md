## Other facts

- `FieldContainer::fc_CanEnterField(p, map, levelType)` FUN_00462380 and `(p, ShinePlayer* t)` FUN_004621f0 return only
  `ENTER_MAP_ERR_NONE / DATA_NULL / LEVEL_LIMIT`; on a mover they require p to drive it and test every passenger's
  level. Z08 §10 calls FUN_004621f0 the "local summon"; it is this check (Z30 §6.2).
- `WELLRNG512a` FUN_0063c8c0 is the unmodified reference WELL512a (checked numerically); its only users are the mover
  upgrade rare roll (`CMoverUpgradeData::GetIsRareMover` / `GetRareMoverItemID`, formula in Z30 §9).
- `CParty::GetEmptyParty` FUN_00645070 hands out party numbers round-robin (`m_EmptyPartySearchPoint`), not lowest-free.
- TERMEXTEND (0x3057, Z04 §12.9) writes the new end date into the in-memory item before the DB answer; cap now + 90 days.
- The holy-promise money transfer over the ring (0x9436 → 0x941B) has no originator in the 2016 zone (empty hook).
- `0xB40F` (WM → every zone after a rename) only edits the zone's party / raid copy; `bInit` removes the member.
