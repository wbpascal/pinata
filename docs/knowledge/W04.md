## Answers to other items' open questions

- **C01 §5:** `0x083A` is NC_MISC_EVENT_DONE_MUNSANG_WM2Z *(pdb2017)*, 13 bytes, sent by FUN_00408050 to a **zone** only;
  clients get 0x6402 from the same function. The whole chain is dead in 2016 (W04-B56, §17). `0x7C07` = NC_PRISON_GET_ACK
  (§9.3). `0x5802`, `0x7038`, `0x703A` are sent to clients and ignored by the 2016 client; `0xA415` goes to the Character
  server, not a client (§13.3).
- **Z06 §8:** the WM sender of 0xBC09 NC_GAMBLE_EXCHANGEDCOIN_INIT_CMD is `CGambleSystemServer` (§15.1): reset time from
  GBHouse.shn row 0, checked on every main-loop pass.
- **Z08:** the WM waits 1 min for 0x580E (Status 1 → 9); a late 0x580E → Status 0x0B + 0x5811. 0x5819 is never sent by the
  2016 WM. KQ start times come from KingdomQuest.shn (day ST_Day of the current month at ST_Hour:ST_Minute, every
  NextStartDeleyMin minutes; the next two are always listed, §6.2). The zone sends 0xA401 / 0xA403 from `mc_Routine`
  FUN_004868d0 / FUN_00486710 / `mc_AllDropFilm` FUN_00485290, which Z08 §5 does not cover (they are in Z14's goal); 0x0810 {1, 300} is the 300-s
  countdown in `KQuestTimeWin`. Z08 §12 prison-go codes are 0xDC0 ok / 0xDC1 charNo mismatch / 0xDC2 `so_Prison_Go`
  failed (zone FUN_005a4470), not what Z08's text says. Z08 §13 (zone GM events) can cite §16 for the WM side; deleting
  an active event never reaches the zones (W04-B50).
- **W01:** `g_InstanceMaps` is the `CKQ` object (above). §5.4's FUN_004589b0 / FUN_00458790 "name registry" is
  `CPrisonServer_SaveOnLogout` (§9.1). §4.2's last 0x1004 field ("stored day-start time − 1 day") is
  `g_GambleSystemServer.nextReset − 1 day`, i.e. the last gamble reset (§15.1). FUN_00452e40 / FUN_00454ee0 in 0x1005 are
  `KQ_RejoinOnCharData` / `KQ_IsJoinerBanned`; the banned case sets session +0x1DF74 (§6.1). The `MsgWorldManager.shn`
  table loaded first by FUN_00404100 holds the KQ notice texts. W01 §11's 0x085F summary: the report also feeds a GM
  alert (0x200D, §2.5).
- **D04:** the client texts for 0x949 / 0x952 (§3) are "DB Error" (client literal), not what D04 quotes. D04 §7's "other
  failures" text for 0x7C0A is wrong: the client shows nothing for 0xDA7 (W04-B27). D04 §10's chat-restrict codes match
  the WM relay; the client shows text only for 0x1C03 / 0x1C04 / 0x1C07 / 0x1C08 (W04-B46).
- **D02 §16:** the sender of 0x0841 is the WM's client 0x0840 handler FUN_00417140 (§17.3).
- **W01 §11 / §6.3 / §7.6 hand-offs** (0x0840, 0x0844, 0x0846, 0x085F, 0x0861, 0x0864, 0x0866, 0x0869, 0x10EC, 0x10EF,
  0x10F1, 0x10F4, attendance manager, NPC schedule) are documented in §17.2–§17.6. The 0x180C "attendance: if flagged"
  step also sends zone 0x1120 NC_CHAR_SUPPORT_REWARD_CMD {charNo, userKind} (FUN_00433ab0, §17.5).
- **Z11:** zone `&releasechatban` (GM-command parser FUN_00425b70, send helper FUN_00421a60) sends 0x0866 {GM charNo, name}; the WM checks the GM's adminlevel
  50…100 and relays to CharDB 0x0868; the result returns as 0x0865 to the target's zone and 0x0867 to the GM (§17.4).
- **Account server item:** `fc_NC_MISC_EVENT_DONE_MUNSANG_WM2ACC` (account FUN_00402cb0) answers 0x0839 with a fixed
  nError 0x1545 and no DB call (§17).

## Notes for a rebuild

- The WM never filters chat with the chat-block list; the client does (§14).
- A chat ban is CHARDATA single option `nChatBan` (≠ 0 = banned, the WM writes 0x0FFFFFFF); the zone copy comes from 0x0865.
- The 2016 client ignores nError in 0x10F2 (W04-B64): a rebuild should not send 0x10F2 for refused reward requests or
  must accept that the client shows them as received.
- The KQ notices (0x580B) are not shown by the 2016 in-game client (no handler in the game scene).
- Instance ranking (§13.3) is dead in 2016: the Character server sends none of its inputs.
