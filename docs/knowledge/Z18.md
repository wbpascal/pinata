## Facts other items need

- **Client session modes** (`ClientSession::SessionMode` *(pdb2016)*): −1 INACTIVE, 0 BEFORE_MAP_LOGIN_REQ (set at
  accept), 1 BEFORE_MAP_LOGINCOMPLETE_CMD (at 0x1801), 2 AFTER_MAP_LOGINCOMPLETE_CMD (at 0x1803). Z18 §1.3.
- **Per-IP limit**: at most 9 client sessions of one IP may be between accept and 0x1803; the 10th closes all 10
  (`csm_AddClientSessionToIPGroup` FUN_0045c720). Z18 §1.3.
- **Double login on one zone**: `sp_KeepFromDoubleLogin` FUN_0043cc90 bans every other player object with the same
  user number (account); `sp_GhostBan` FUN_0043f3d0 logs out of sight every player of the map with the same name.
- **ClientSession members** *(pdb2016)*: +0x100 `cs_sessionhandle`, +0x102 `cs_clienthandle` (player handle),
  +0x104 `cs_sessionMode`, +0x108 `cs_activeTime`, +0x10c `cs_bClosingSession`; peer IP at +0x34 (inside `m_SocketIOCP`).

  `f_SystemRemove`; `aeo_Tick` (+0x1c) = `{start, terminate, Metronome nextaction}`.
