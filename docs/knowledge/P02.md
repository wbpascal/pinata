## Facts

- Login and WM seeds: per-server counter `(prev + 1) % 499`, initial `rand()` (Login ctor @ FUN_00409680,
  `ActiveSession` @ FUN_00409700). Zone: per-process, small (1, 2, …) *(inferred: counter from 0)*.
- `0x0C0C`/`0x0C0D` validate key: XOR of the 32 words = per-world counter `(+1) & 0x7FF`
  (`GetClientValidation` @ FUN_0040a200); the 31 random words are 15-bit.
- The captures contain no SYN/ACK frames (saved as displayed packets); the decoder's resync at offset 0 no longer
  discards a captured seed (fixed in `pcap_decode.py` by P02).
