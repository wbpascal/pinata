## Formula (one attacker unit, share `S = trunc(MonEXP · (damage / totalDamage))`)

- Solo unit, or party/raid with exactly one member near the mob: `e_Attacker` gets `trunc(S · lh4ed(...))`.
- ≥ 2 members near (party: `EXPRange`; raid: 1000 units; no alive check): base `trunc(((L+5)²/Σ(L+5)²)·S)` for all;
  bonus `B = trunc(S·(f−1))`, `f = pebd_Bonus[v]`, v = members within 20 levels of the top one, split by `L²` among
  those; cut above `megag_ExpLimit[L]` and hand the cut out again by `L²` (no second cap); **no level handicap**.
- Quotient before product, as written (`100·(29/50)` → 57, not 58). Every receiver gets at least 1 EXP
  (`so_GetExperienceFromMob` FUN_004302a0 floors `(int)exp < 1` to 1), solo players with handicap 0 included.
- Every double→int conversion truncates toward zero (x87 `fistp` under RC = 11, or `_ftol2_sse`), although Ghidra
  prints `ROUND`. Applies to Z03 §8.2's `round(...)` too.

## `HitMeList::Enemy.e_Attacker`

Always a player (servants are mapped to their master in `el_StoreDamage` FUN_00482f00; only players are stored). For a
party/raid it is the member whose `el_vecEnemyPlayer` entry is last (filled at the kill by `el_StoreLastDamage`
FUN_00483030 → `el_Store` FUN_00482070 in vector order), not necessarily the latest hitter.

## Corrections to done items (for X01)

- Z03 §8.2: group members (n ≥ 2) get no level handicap; members are not checked for being alive; the bonus index is
  the number of bonus-valid members, not of all members; conversions truncate (Z29 §2.6).
